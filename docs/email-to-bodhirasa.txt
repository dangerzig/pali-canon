I hope this message finds you well. I'm writing to share some findings from a digital critical edition project I've been working on, which may be useful for the Digital Pali Dictionary.


ABOUT THE PROJECT

I've been developing a computational critical edition of the complete Pali Tipitaka, collating three independent textual witnesses:

  - GRETIL (PTS edition transcriptions)
  - VRI (Chattha Sangayana CST4)
  - SuttaCentral (Mahasangiti edition)

As part of this work, I used DPD (version 0.3.20260202) to lemmatize the entire SuttaCentral corpus -- approximately 1.6 million word tokens representing 127,032 unique word forms.


LEMMATIZATION RESULTS

The results were excellent:

  - Coverage: 97.7% (124,105 of 127,032 unique words identified)
  - Sandhi decompositions: 42,449
  - Orthographic variant normalizations (-n/-m to -m with niggahita): 1,555
  - Metrical variant normalizations: 2,154+
  - DPPN proper noun matches: 21

This is a testament to the comprehensiveness of DPD!


ADDITIONAL PATTERNS WE HANDLED

Beyond DPD's built-in lookup, our lemmatizer handles several predictable patterns:

  - Jhana compounds (3): pathamajhana, dutiyajhana, tatiyajhana
  - Chapter/story titles (23): hamcivagga, sereyyavagga
  - Apadana titles (144): korandapupphiyattheraapadana
  - Causative absolutives (79): abhinivajjayitva, avattthapetva
  - Pronoun-verb fusions (23): dubbalohamasmi, ayahamasmi
  - Short pronoun variants (1): m->mam, tan->tam


UNKNOWN WORDS

After filtering handled patterns, 2,983 unique word forms remain unidentified. These appear to be genuine candidates for DPD consideration.


Distribution:

97% come from the Khuddaka Nikaya (2,888 words):

  - Culaniddesa/Mahaniddesa: technical commentary terms
  - Jataka: narrative vocabulary, proper nouns
  - Apadana: verse forms, hagiographic terms
  - Milindapanha: philosophical/debate vocabulary
  - Netti/Petakopadesa: hermeneutical technical terms

Only 95 unknowns from DN/MN/SN/AN combined.


Examples of Potential New Headwords:

From Netti/Petakopadesa (technical terms):
  - samaaropano (36x) - technical term
  - otarano (26x) - technical term

From Nikayas (rare but interesting):

  - ayokhilam (25x across AN/KN) - iron stake
  - osaariyamaanaani (7x in DN) - being led away
