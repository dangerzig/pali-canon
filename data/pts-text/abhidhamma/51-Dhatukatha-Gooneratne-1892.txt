PREFACE. 


I am answerable for the final division into words, sentences, 
and paragraphs; for the numbering of the paragraphs in 
the text ; for the references to those paragraphs inserted in 
the commentary ; for the underlining of the actual words 
quoted. I have also drawn up the index, and corrected the 
proofs. While doing this I have collated two MSS., both 
in Sinhalese characters, one of the text and comment (Sd), 
and one of the text only (Sb.).
